# RecoverSense — Metrics Specification

Metrics are computed from system records/audit data.

## Revenue at risk

Total amount across failed payments entering the system for the measured run.

## Verified recovered revenue

Amount for cases whose resulting business state was independently verified as recovered.

## Recovery rate

`verified recovered revenue / revenue at risk`

Also report per strategy where useful.

## Diagnosis accuracy

Predicted failure type vs labeled ground truth on synthetic test data.

## Strategy accuracy

Predicted strategy vs labeled optimal strategy on synthetic test data.

## Avoided unnecessary attempts

Cases routed to STOP or ESCALATE instead of an unnecessary retry.

## Policy violations blocked

Proposed actions rejected by deterministic policy and the rejection reason.

## Verification success rate

`verified successful executions / executed actions`

## Dashboard warning

The reference mockup contains example numbers such as ₹62,000 at risk and ₹47,500 recovered. These are visual mockup values, not project results. Never present them as actual RecoverSense performance unless generated by the real system.
